# v0.3.0 Outcome-Optimized Recommendations

- **Target version:** v0.3.0
- **Predecessor:** [v0.2.1 Quality-First Ranking and Validation](v0.2.1-quality-first-ranking-validation.md)

## Objective

Deliver a daily recommendation workflow whose primary measurable objective is the number of papers
the user explicitly marks worthwhile after reading, while preserving quality floors, privacy,
inspectability, bounded cost, and safe recovery.

## Scope

- Distinguish pre-reading interest actions, completed reading, and explicit worthwhile or
  not-worthwhile post-reading outcomes.
- Measure worthwhile reads per published batch without treating silence, delayed reading, or missing
  feedback as negative evidence.
- Expand candidate coverage beyond fixed core and one-hop adjacent categories through controlled,
  privacy-bounded cross-category discovery.
- Rank candidates for expected worthwhile reading while keeping personal relevance, scientific
  quality, uncertainty, and evidence provenance separately inspectable.
- Admit at most one bounded exploration recommendation by default when it satisfies the declared
  quality and risk requirements; never force an ineligible exploration item into a batch.
- Record privacy-safe exposure and policy evidence sufficient to compare candidate policies against
  the released v0.2.1 baseline before activation.

## Non-Goals

- Optimizing clicks, saves, opens, reading completion, novelty, category coverage, or model scores as
  substitutes for explicit post-reading worthwhileness.
- Treating absent feedback, an unsubmitted browser action, or an unread paper as a negative outcome.
- Sending raw Zotero records, notes, annotations, PDF content, collection names, feedback prose, or
  protected profile terms to a remote provider.
- Allowing a model to control candidate identities, URLs, persistence, publication, exploration
  limits, activation, or security policy.
- Automatically activating a learned policy from sparse, biased, unversioned, or unreviewed feedback.
- Weakening the v0.2.1 scientific-value gates, encrypted state, previous-site preservation, schema
  readers, or v0.1.2 rollback path.

## Milestones

### V030-M1 Explicit Post-Reading Outcomes

The product collects reading completion separately from an explicit worthwhile or not-worthwhile
judgment and preserves corrections and delayed feedback.

**Acceptance:** Browser, contract, and persistence tests cover every pre-reading and post-reading
action, invalid transitions, corrections, duplicate submission, delayed submission, repeated papers,
legacy feedback, and empty feedback. Worthwhile and not-worthwhile outcomes are accepted only as
explicit actions, and silence remains unknown.

### V030-M2 Outcome Measurement

Every successful publication can be evaluated by worthwhile reads per batch, reading completions,
worthwhile outcomes conditional on reading, and explicit-feedback coverage without exposing private
content.

**Acceptance:** Deterministic tests cover position-aware impressions, late outcomes, partial labels,
repeated batches, corrections, missing feedback, and zero denominators. Reports reproduce the exact
event ledger totals, never infer negative outcomes, and contain only allowlisted aggregate fields.

### V030-M3 Controlled Candidate Coverage

Candidate discovery includes bounded cross-category papers that share a relevant problem, method, or
task with the protected profile, while retaining deterministic limits and failure recovery.

**Acceptance:** Synthetic fixtures demonstrate recall of declared cross-category bridge papers that
the v0.2.1 category path cannot retrieve. Tests cover empty profiles, malformed metadata, duplicate
candidates, timeouts, partial failure, bounded calls and pool size, stable ordering, and fallback to
the previous usable candidate pool without publishing private profile content.

### V030-M4 Worthwhile-Reading Selection

Selection prioritizes the expected number of worthwhile reads in the daily batch. Personal reading
likelihood and post-reading value remain separately reviewable, and confident scientific-value
failures remain ineligible.

**Acceptance:** Frozen offline fixtures cover high-interest low-value, low-interest high-value,
missing-evidence, delayed-feedback, and conflicting-signal cases. The selected batch maximizes the
declared worthwhile-reading objective subject to its published constraints, produces calibrated
bounded outputs, preserves deterministic ties, and cannot be reordered or expanded by model output.

### V030-M5 Safe Exploration

Exploration targets uncertain potentially worthwhile papers rather than category difference alone,
and consumes no more than the configured risk budget.

**Acceptance:** Deterministic tests prove the default batch contains at most one exploration item,
never forces an ineligible item, respects quality and publication-history constraints, records its
selection evidence, and fills the slot with the best eligible non-exploration candidate when no safe
exploration candidate exists. Repeated runs remain deterministic for a fixed policy seed.

### V030-M6 Activation and Release Safety

Candidate policies remain shadow-only until their outcome evidence is sufficient for explicit
operator approval, and the released v0.2.1 policy remains immediately restorable.

**Acceptance:** Policy comparison uses versioned exposure probabilities and explicit post-reading
outcomes, reports uncertainty and sample insufficiency, and refuses automatic activation. Formatting,
lint, strict typing, offline tests, migrations, package and static-site builds, browser tests,
artifact inspection, production canary, failure recovery, and rollback pass without private-content
exposure.

## Stage Acceptance

v0.3.0 is releasable when V030-M1 through V030-M6 pass, the operator confirms the production canary
collects and reports explicit worthwhile outcomes correctly, and any outcome-adaptive policy is either
explicitly approved from sufficient comparison evidence or remains disabled behind the released
v0.2.1-compatible policy.
