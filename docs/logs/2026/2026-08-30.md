# 2026-08-30

## Summary

- Implemented V030-M4 worthwhile-reading selection on `feature/v0.3-worthwhile-selection`, stacked
  on the serving-profile slice. The scheduled production path remains on the released v0.2.1
  relevance objective; the new objective is reachable only through offline evaluation and the CLI.

## Added

- Added a declared, versioned worthwhile objective that estimates expected worthwhile reads as
  reading likelihood multiplied by post-reading value, keeping both factors and their evidence
  confidence separately inspectable per candidate.
- Added affine-plus-shrinkage factor calibration with declared floor, ceiling, and prior, so every
  estimate stays bounded and monotone and zero-confidence evidence returns the prior rather than a
  zero.
- Added an `expected_worthwhile` selection objective that reorders the qualified pool only, leaving
  minimum score, judged quality, confident scientific-value rejection, source quotas, author and
  topic diversity, and the batch target unchanged.
- Added an offline objective report comparing declared per-batch predictions with realized explicit
  outcomes, reporting sample size, coverage, unlabeled impressions, and a proposed but unapproved
  calibration.
- Added an `evaluate worthwhile` command writing an owner-only aggregate report to
  `runtime/worthwhile-report.json`.
- Recorded the objective, its declared constants, and the activation boundary in ADR-0011.

## Changed

- Kept `SelectionPolicy` defaulting to the relevance objective, so `pipeline/recommend.py` and all
  three workflows continue to produce the exact released v0.2.1 selection.

## Refactored

- Promoted per-group feature aggregation from `ranking/select.py` into `ranking/weights.py` as
  `group_value` for confidence-folded relevance scoring and `aggregate_groups` for objective
  estimation, which needs value and confidence separable. Both share one importance table, so the
  two consumers cannot drift apart. Relevance scoring is unchanged.

## Tests and Verification

- Repository-wide `ruff format --check`, `ruff check`, and strict `mypy` over 124 source files
  passed, and 311 offline tests passed.
- Frozen fixtures cover the five named V030-M4 cases: high-interest low-value, low-interest
  high-value, missing evidence, delayed feedback, and conflicting signal.
- Additional coverage verifies normalized bounds, that the expected value is exactly the product of
  its two factors, deterministic canonical tie-breaking across repeated runs, that supplied
  estimates can neither expand a batch beyond its target nor admit a paper rejected by a confident
  scientific-value gate, and that the default policy reproduces relevance selection exactly.
- The written report was verified to be owner-only, free of paper identifiers, and stable across
  repeated evaluation of identical inputs.
- The `0.3.0` source distribution and wheel built successfully in temporary storage and the artifact
  safety check passed.

## Compatibility and Migration

- No persisted schema changed and no workflow file was modified. `pipeline/recommend.py`,
  `security/state.py`, and all three workflow YAML files were deliberately left untouched.
- `runtime/worthwhile-report.json` is ignored local output outside the encrypted state allowlist, so
  it is never restored, published, or migrated. Deleting it is a complete rollback.
- The declared objective policy is a constant, not a persisted registry. Versioned activation
  remains V030-M6's scope; the policy version is recorded in every report.

## Follow-ups

- The objective is not wired into the production pipeline and has no measured outcome improvement
  yet. Activation requires V030-M6 policy comparison and an explicit operator decision.
- Per-batch predictions are not persisted at publication time, so the report currently compares
  realized outcomes against predictions only when they are supplied explicitly. Recording them
  alongside impressions is the natural input to V030-M5 exploration and M6 comparison.
- Declared priors are conservative and unvalidated. The proposed calibration in the report is the
  intended review input once explicit post-reading outcomes reach the declared sample threshold.

## Summary

- Implemented V030-M5 safe exploration on `feature/v0.3-safe-exploration`, stacked on the worthwhile
  selection slice. Exploration now targets resolvable uncertainty about worthwhileness instead of
  category difference, and spends a declared risk budget. The scheduled production path is untouched
  and never explores.

## Added

- Added `ranking/exploration.py` with a declared `bounded-uncertainty-v1` policy. A candidate earns
  a slot only when post-reading-value evidence already exists at sufficient confidence, its
  optimistic bound is promising, and its interval is still wide, so a settled off-category paper and
  a paper nothing is known about are both refused.
- Added interval widening by unresolved evidence against the same reviewed `declared-prior-v1`
  calibration, so a widened bound can never leave the declared interval, and derived the potential,
  conservative, and uncertainty values every eligibility rule is stated in.
- Added a declared risk budget in expected worthwhile reads. A slot is priced against the best
  estimate in the pool, which over-states the true displacement, so the budget binds conservatively
  and refusals surface as `cost_exceeds_risk_budget` rather than as silent conservatism.
- Added an inspectable `ExplorationDecision` recording the spend, considered and eligible counts,
  the bounds behind each admitted paper, and the distinct reasons every other candidate was refused.
- Added deterministic seed rotation over candidates whose potential agrees to two decimals, so a
  fixed seed reproduces a batch exactly while a different seed can reach an equally promising paper.
- Recorded the exploration model, its declared constants, and the activation boundary in ADR-0012.

## Changed

- Made a supplied exploration decision own the whole off-category quota, so a batch cannot hold more
  off-category papers than the declared budget under either reading of the word.

## Refactored

- Promoted batch eligibility out of `select_diverse` into a public `qualified_candidates`, so
  ordinary selection and exploration share one definition rather than restating minimum score,
  judged quality, and confident scientific-value rejection separately. This is what makes "never
  forces an ineligible item" structural instead of only tested. Selection behavior is unchanged.

## Tests and Verification

- Repository-wide `ruff format --check`, `ruff check`, and strict `mypy` over 126 source files
  passed, and 333 offline tests passed.
- Deterministic fixtures cover every V030-M5 acceptance clause: at most one exploration item in a
  default batch, no ineligible item forced, quality and publication-history constraints respected,
  selection evidence recorded, the slot returning to ordinary selection when no safe candidate
  exists, and repeated runs identical for a fixed seed.
- Additional coverage verifies that category difference alone earns nothing, that a paper without
  value evidence is refused rather than admitted for having a wide interval, that an estimate from
  another objective policy version is refused, that a batch cannot grow beyond its target, that a
  pick outside the qualified pool is dropped rather than forced, and that admitted intervals stay
  bounded and contain their expected value.
- The `0.3.0` source distribution and wheel built successfully in temporary storage and the artifact
  safety check passed.

## Compatibility and Migration

- No persisted schema changed and no workflow file was modified. `pipeline/recommend.py`,
  `security/state.py`, and all three workflow YAML files were deliberately left untouched.
- `ExplorationDecision` is passed explicitly and defaults to absent, so `select_diverse` without one
  reproduces the released v0.2.1 selection exactly. Rollback is not passing a decision.

## Follow-ups

- Eligibility requires existing post-reading-value evidence, so exploration cannot reach areas the
  local evidence pipeline never assesses. Widening that reach needs a declared risk model for
  unassessed papers, not a relaxed threshold.
- Exploration is not wired into the production pipeline and has no measured outcome improvement yet.
  Choosing a seed rotation cadence and activating a policy remain V030-M6's scope.
- Per-batch predictions are still not persisted at publication time, so exploration spend cannot yet
  be compared against realized outcomes. Recording predictions alongside impressions remains the
  prerequisite for that comparison.
